# Fovoz

**Democratizing investment for all — equity & bond investing starting at ₹10/week, with AI chatbot assistance.**

🔗 **Live app**: [fovoz-insight-grow.lovable.app](https://fovoz-insight-grow.lovable.app)

Presented by R S Vishal, II Computer Science & Engineering, V.S.B Engineering College.

---

## The Problem

Most Indian citizens fear investing:

- **Feels like gambling** — equity and bonds are seen as risky, unpredictable, and "not for people like us."
- **High entry barrier** — most platforms assume users already have savings, salary slips, and market know-how.
- **No one to guide them** — first-time investors have questions at every step but no simple, trustworthy place to ask.
- **Trust deficit** — people have interest but lack trust.

| Stat | Value |
|---|---|
| Indian households investing in securities (SEBI, 2025) | 9.5% |
| US households investing in stocks | 55% (5x India's rate) |
| Financial cushion for most first-time earners to "start big" | ₹0 |

## The Solution

1. **₹10/Week Micro-SIP** — auto-debit as little as ten rupees weekly into a diversified basket of equity and bonds, no lump sum required.
2. **AI Chatbot** — answers questions in plain language, explains every recommendation, and nudges users to stay consistent.
3. **Bank-Grade Security** — RBI/SEBI-aligned KYC, encrypted transactions, and regulated partner brokers protect every rupee.
4. **Progress You Can See** — a simple dashboard shows savings growth and, at a national level, the collective impact of small investors.

### How Fovoz Earns Trust

| Problem | How Fovoz Responds | Outcome for the User |
|---|---|---|
| Fear & distrust of markets | AI chatbot explains every term and trade in plain language, on demand | Confidence replaces confusion |
| Can't afford to "start big" | ₹10-per-week micro-SIP model with no minimum lump sum | Investing becomes a habit, not a hurdle |
| Low financial literacy | Bite-sized lessons and alerts delivered inside the chat itself | Users learn as they invest |
| Feels disconnected from impact | Dashboard links personal savings to national investment growth | A sense of shared national purpose |

## App Architecture

```
User Mobile App
  └── Onboarding • KYC • Weekly ₹10+ auto-invest • Portfolio dashboard

AI Chatbot Layer
  └── NLP query handling • Plain-language guidance • Nudges & reminders

Investment Engine
  └── SIP scheduler • Equity/bond allocation logic • Order routing

Security & Compliance
  └── Encryption • RBI/SEBI KYC-AML checks • Fraud monitoring

External Integrations
  └── Partner broker & NSE/BSE APIs • Bank UPI rails • Market data feeds
```

## Impact

Widening domestic capital participation in sectors like agriculture, IT services, textiles, and manufacturing exports strengthens financial inclusion at a national scale. Every ₹10 invested is a step toward a wealthier, more self-reliant India — deepening capital markets while lifting millions out of poverty.
